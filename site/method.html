How the decision policy works

The controller scores admissible candidates using 0.4 × information_gain + 0.3 × impact + 0.2 × feasibility − 0.15 × cost − 0.25 × risk. These inputs are host judgments, not measured probabilities or scientific confidence. A candidate with unmet prerequisites is rejected.

What the record means

Persistent state makes work resumable and inspectable. Structural checks, artifact hashes, and reviewer labels do not establish scientific truth. Research claims still need evidence and substantive review.
